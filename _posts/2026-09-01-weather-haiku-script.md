I don't check the weather. I just like that it runs.

Last Tuesday I was bored, so I wrote a Ruby script that generates haikus about the weather in my city. It pulls the current temperature from a public API, picks a random adjective ("muggy", "crisp", "unseasonably warm"), and feeds it into a haiku template.

```
The air is muggy
Windows fog at 7 PM
Summer is still here
```

Are they good haikus? Not really. Do I check the weather now? No. But the script runs every hour on a server in my closet, and that's enough.

I don't know why I keep doing this. Most of my ideas come to me when I'm bored on a Tuesday. Most of them never go anywhere. None of them are useful. I prefer it that way.
